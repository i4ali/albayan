Privacy Policy

AlBayan: Quran & Tafsir · Effective May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the AlBayan: Quran & Tafsir mobile application (the “App”) handles information. The App is operated by Muhammad Imran Ali (“we”, “our”).

Summary: AlBayan does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data brokers. All your reading progress, bookmarks, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device and never sent anywhere.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials. The App does not require you to create an account.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Sentry, Crashlytics, or similar).
  • We do not display advertising and do not share data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites and do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework.

2. Information stored locally on your device

The App stores the following data only on your device, using Apple’s standard UserDefaults and the local file system. This data is never transmitted to us:

  • Bookmarks and saved verses
  • Reading progress (which surahs and verses you have read)
  • Quiz scores and progress
  • Audio playback preferences (selected reciter, playback speed, repeat mode)
  • Theme and language preferences
  • Notification preferences and locally-scheduled reminder times
  • Cached audio files used for offline recitation playback

You can erase this data at any time by deleting the App from your device.

3. Location

If you enable prayer-time features, the App may request access to your device’s current location (When In Use). Location is used solely on your device to calculate accurate prayer times based on your geographical position. Your location is never transmitted to us, never stored on any server, and never shared with any third party. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

4. Third-party audio sources

The App streams Qur’an recitation audio from public third-party content delivery networks:

  • mp3quran.net – recitations by Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Abdurrahmaan As-Sudais, Saad Al-Ghamadi, Ahmed Al-Ajamy, Maher Al-Muaiqly, and Yasser Ad-Dussary.
  • everyayah.com – verse-level audio for the same reciters.

When the App requests audio from these services, the host may receive standard HTTP request information (your IP address and a generic User-Agent string), as is the case with any web request. We do not control the privacy practices of these third parties; please consult their respective terms.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Premium features are sold via Apple’s in-app purchase system (StoreKit). All payment information is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details. We rely solely on the cryptographically signed entitlement information provided by StoreKit on your device to determine whether premium features should be unlocked.

6. Notifications

The App uses Apple’s local notification framework (UNUserNotificationCenter) to schedule reminders on your device. We do not use push notifications, do not register a push token, and do not send notifications from any server. All reminder notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not collect any personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through any channel, please contact us so we can investigate.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no server-side data retention. All on-device data described in Section 2 can be removed by deleting the App or clearing app data through iOS Settings › General › iPhone Storage.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the GDPR in the EU/EEA, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA in California, and similar laws), you may have rights regarding your personal information. Because we do not collect or process any personal information, these rights have nothing to apply to in our case. If you have any concern, you may contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after a change indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
